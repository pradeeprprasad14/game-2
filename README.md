# ⚖️ Tarazu: The Balance Scale Game (Classes 1-3)
### पारंपारिक भारतीय तराजू खेळ | पारंपरिक भारतीय तराजू का खेल

An interactive, responsive 2D drag-and-drop educational game that brings the traditional Indian balance scale (*tarazu* / वजन काटा) to life. Designed specifically for early primary school learners (Class 1–3) to develop number sense, addition, comparison, and foundational algebraic thinking through joyful play.

### 2. Traditional Shaped Brass *Batta* Weights (Right Pan)
Each weight denomination has an iconic, historic Indian geometric shape:
- **1 kg**: **Hexagon (षट्कोण)** — Classic 6-sided brass prism with central knob and calibration stamp circle.
- **2 kg**: **Octagon (अष्टकोण)** — 8-sided faceted prism with concentric stamp rings.
- **3 kg**: **Conical Bell (घंटाकार)** — Traditional Indian bell-metal tapered weight with top brass hoop handle.
- **4 kg**: **Decagon (दशकोण)** — 10-sided faceted stepped heavy brass block.
- **5 kg**: **Heavy Master Hexagon (महा-षट्कोण)** — Wide heavy brass/iron block with center well and star stamp.
- **6 kg**: **Heavy Stepped Pillar (स्तंभाकार)** — Heavy faceted master block.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Open `index.html`** in any web browser on tablet, phone, or laptop.
2. **Choose Mascot & Language:** Pick Mithoo 🦜, Chintu 🐒, or Gappu 🐘.
3. **Listen to Spoken Hints:** Tap 🔊 to hear prompts spoken in a warm human teacher's voice.
4. **Weigh the Object:** Drag or tap shaped brass *batta* weights from the tray to the right pan until the red needle points straight up and the jewel glows emerald green!
