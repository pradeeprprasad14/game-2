# ⚖️ Tarazu: The Balance Scale Game (Classes 1-3)
### पारंपारिक भारतीय तराजू खेळ | पारंपरिक भारतीय तराजू का खेल

An interactive, responsive 2D drag-and-drop educational game that brings the traditional Indian balance scale (*tarazu* / वजन काटा) to life. Designed specifically for early primary school learners (Class 1–3) to develop number sense, addition, comparison, and foundational algebraic thinking through joyful play.

---

## 🦁 NEW: Animal Kingdom Section (प्राण्यांचे राज्य / पशु साम्राज्य)

Children can now enter a lush Jungle Safari and balance cute cartoon animals on **EITHER side of the Tarazu**!

### 🐾 Cute Cartoon Animals & Weights:
- 🐘 **Elephant (हाथी / हत्ती)**: **100 kg** — Majestic gentle giant with brassy trumpet sound!
- 🦁 **Lion (शेर / सिंह)**: **50 kg** — King of the jungle with cartoon roar!
- 🐒 **Monkey (बंदर / माकड)**: **10 kg** — Playful acrobatic friend with cheerful chatter chirps!
- 🐰 **Rabbit (खरगोश / ससा)**: **5 kg** — Fluffy hopper with sweet boing squeak!

### 🎯 Math & Early Algebra Curriculum in Animal Kingdom:
- **Doubling & Halving:**
  - 1 Monkey (10 kg) = 2 Rabbits (5 kg + 5 kg)
  - 1 Elephant (100 kg) = 2 Lions (50 kg + 50 kg)
- **Skip Counting & Multiplication Concepts:**
  - 1 Lion (50 kg) = 5 Monkeys (10 kg × 5)
  - 1 Lion (50 kg) = 10 Rabbits (5 kg × 10)
  - 1 Elephant (100 kg) = 10 Monkeys (10 kg × 10) = 20 Rabbits (5 kg × 20)
- **Number Decomposition & Addition:**
  - Balance 50 kg with mixed combinations (e.g., 3 Monkeys + 4 Rabbits = 30 + 20 = 50 kg).
- **Two-Sided Balancing (Seesaw Equations):**
  - Drag animals onto **either the Left Pan or Right Pan**!
  - 1 Elephant (100 kg) + 1 Monkey (10 kg) = 2 Lions (100 kg) + 2 Rabbits (10 kg).
- **Free Safari Playground:**
  - Open-ended sandbox where children freely drag, drop, and compare weights with real-time feedback.

### 🔊 Studio-Quality Human Voice Audio & Procedural Sound Effects:
- **Authentic Human Voice (No Robotic Synthesis):** Pre-rendered high-fidelity human teacher voices in English (`en-IN`), Hindi (`hi-IN`), and Marathi (`mr-IN`) for all 14 Bazaar levels, all 6 Animal Kingdom challenges, welcome speeches, hints, and celebration victories!
- **Speaking Mascot Animation:** Mascot avatar bounces and animates expressively while speaking.
- **Procedural Animal Sounds (Web Audio API):**
  - 🎺 Elephant Trumpet (rich harmonic sweep + vibrato LFO)
  - 🦁 Lion Roar (low-frequency sawtooth + formant bandpass filter)
  - 🐒 Monkey Chatter (rapid triple-tone ascending chirps)
  - 🐰 Rabbit Squeak (springy high-frequency whistle hop)
- **Temple Bell:** Resonant harmonic brass chime on entry and balance!

## 🎮 How to Play
1. **Open `index.html`** in any web browser on phone, tablet, or PC.
2. **Choose Mode:** Tap `🛒 ENTER BAZAAR` for classic produce & brass weights, or `🦁 ANIMAL KINGDOM` for cartoon animal balancing!
3. **Switch Anytime:** Use the top bar pill `[🛒 Bazaar] [🐾 Animals]` to jump between sections instantly without losing progress.
4. **Choose Mascot & Language:** Mithoo 🦜, Chintu 🐒, or Gappu 🐘 in English, हिन्दी, or मराठी.
5. **Listen to Spoken Hints:** Tap 🔊 to hear hints spoken in a warm human voice.
6. **Balance the Scale:** Drag or tap animals or weights onto the pans until the red needle points straight up and the emerald jewel glows green!
